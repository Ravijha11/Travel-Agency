# Legal docs (minimum set)

This repo is a **coordination-only** directory, so the legal wording should clearly state:
- the platform is not a transport operator
- riders and drivers coordinate directly
- payments are handled off-platform
- safety and verification meaning are described

## Pages to publish on the website
- **Terms of Service**
- **Privacy Policy**
- **Disclaimer**
- **Driver Agreement**

## Recommended sections (outline)
### Terms of Service
- Eligibility
- Coordination-only nature (no booking guarantee)
- User responsibilities (rider + driver)
- Prohibited use
- Suspension/termination
- Limitation of liability
- Contact details

### Privacy Policy
- Data collected (name, phone, car details, auth identifiers)
- How you use data (display listings, anti-abuse, support)
- Sharing (public display of driver contact only for active trips)
- Cookies/session
- Retention and deletion requests
- Contact details

### Disclaimer
- Safety and personal responsibility
- Verification is a trust signal, not a guarantee
- Fares are agreed directly

### Driver Agreement
- Accurate listing information
- Conduct and anti-harassment
- No illegal or unsafe behavior
- Verification and document checks (if you do them)
- Suspension rules
